2. **TNF** - Pro-inflammatory cytokine (5.56 log2FC)
3. **IL6** - Cytokine storm mediator (4.46 log2FC)
4. **CXCL2** - Neutrophil chemotaxis (5.23 log2FC)
5. **IL1A** - Innate immunity bridge (5.73 log2FC)

### Critical Pathways Activated
- Defense response to virus (P = 1.28e-08)
- TNF signaling pathway
- NF-κB signaling (inflammatory cascade)
- IL-17 signaling (T-cell response)
- Interferon-mediated immunity

### Network Hub Genes (Master Regulators)
- **IRF1** - Interferon Regulatory Factor 1 (centrality: 0.861)
- **FOSB** - Transcription factor (centrality: 0.861)
- **CXCL2** - Chemokine hub (centrality: 0.835)
- **NFKBIZ** - NF-κB regulator (centrality: 0.835)

### Therapeutic Targets Identified
